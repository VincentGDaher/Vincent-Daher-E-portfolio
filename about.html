Who I am as a mathematics teacher

Introduction

Mathematics teacher, trainer, and reflective practitioner

I am Vincent Daher, a mathematics educator developing my professional identity through teaching, training, observation, and didactic reflection.

Professional identity

A personalistic vision of education

My teaching identity is rooted in a personalistic vision of education, inspired by Emmanuel Mounier's understanding of the human person. I do not see the student as a simple receiver of knowledge, but as a unique person with dignity, freedom, potential, and a personal vocation.

Teaching, for me, is not only the transmission of mathematical content; it is an act of accompaniment that helps each learner grow intellectually, morally, and humanly.

Teaching

Teaching identity

In the classroom, I seek to create a space where students are challenged to think, to question, to take responsibility for their learning, and to discover their capacity to progress.

Following Mounier's personalism, education becomes a relationship of trust, dialogue, and commitment, where the teacher helps the student become more fully a person within a community.
